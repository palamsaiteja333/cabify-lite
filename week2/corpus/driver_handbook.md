# Driver Handbook

**Document owner:** Driver Operations
**Version:** 7.3
**Effective date:** 15 July 2026
**Audience:** Drivers on the Cabify-Lite platform

Welcome. This handbook is the practical guide to driving on Cabify-Lite: how work is allocated,
what we expect of you, how you are paid, and what happens when things go wrong. Read section 5
(Cancellations) and section 8 (Payouts) carefully — they are the two sections that generate the most
support tickets.

---

## 1. Getting on the platform

### 1.1 Documents

Before your first Ride we verify:

- a valid commercial driving licence;
- vehicle registration in your name or a signed authorisation from the owner;
- commercial passenger insurance, current;
- a fitness certificate and pollution-under-control certificate for the vehicle;
- police verification.

### 1.2 Renewals

Expiring documents are flagged 30 days ahead in the Driver app. **A document that expires takes you
offline automatically.** There is no grace period. Upload renewals early.

### 1.3 Vehicle categories

| Category | Typical vehicles | Seats offered |
|---|---|---|
| Hatchback | Alto, Swift, i10 | 4 |
| Sedan | Dzire, Aura, Amaze | 4 |
| SUV | Ertiga, Carens, Innova | 6 |

Your category is set at onboarding and affects which Bookings you are offered.

## 2. Going online and getting Rides

2.1 You are offered Rides when you are online and within range of a Booking. Allocation considers
proximity, vehicle category, direction of travel, and your acceptance history.

2.2 You have **20 seconds** to accept an offered Ride. Offers that lapse are not counted as
rejections, but a pattern of lapsed offers reduces how often you are offered work.

2.3 There is no minimum number of hours. You decide when to be online.

---

## 3. At the pickup

3.1 Navigate to the pinned location, not to an approximation of it. Most no-show disputes are
decided on whether you were at the pin.

3.2 **Mark arrival in the app when you arrive.** The waiting clock does not start until you do, and
an unmarked arrival will not support a no-show charge.

3.3 Call the Rider once on arrival if they are not visible.

3.4 **Waiting periods:** 5 minutes at a standard pickup, 10 minutes at the airport. These are
minimums you must observe before ending the Booking as a no-show. Ending early invalidates the
charge and the Rider's money is returned.

3.5 Where a valid no-show is recorded, you receive **₹40 of the ₹60 no-show fee** as compensation
for the trip to the pickup.

---

## 4. During the Ride

4.1 Follow the route in the app unless the Rider requests otherwise or conditions make it
impassable. Deviations are visible in the GPS trace and are the first thing we look at in an
overcharge dispute.

4.2 Do not accept cash for a Ride booked as a card or UPI payment.

4.3 Do not ask the Rider to cancel. If you cannot complete the Ride, cancel it yourself and select
the honest reason. Asking a Rider to cancel so that the cancellation lands on their record is a
serious breach and is grounds for deactivation.

4.4 Keep the vehicle clean, the air conditioning functional, and the music at a volume the Rider is
comfortable with.

4.5 Smoking in the vehicle is prohibited, including between Rides.

## 5. Cancellations

### 5.1 When you may cancel

Cancel where you genuinely cannot complete the Ride: a breakdown, an unsafe pickup location, a
medical emergency, or a Rider who is clearly unfit to travel.

### 5.2 When you should not cancel

Do not cancel because the trip is short, because the destination is inconvenient, because the fare
is low, or because you would prefer a different Rider. These are the cancellations that damage the
service most, and they are visible to us by reason code and by pattern.

### 5.3 The threshold

> Your rolling **cancellation rate must stay at or below 8%** of accepted Rides over the trailing
> 30 days.

| Rolling 30-day cancellation rate | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0% – 8% | Normal. No action. |
| Above 8% – 12% | Warning in the Driver app; a coaching call is scheduled. |
| Above 12% for two consecutive weeks | Temporary suspension of 7 days. |
| Above 12% after reinstatement | Permanent deactivation. |

Cancellations caused by a Rider who is unreachable, by a Rider who asks you to cancel, or by a
platform outage are excluded from the calculation when correctly coded.

### 5.4 Rider cancellations

Where a Rider cancels after the grace period, a cancellation fee is charged to the Rider. You
receive **₹20 of the ₹30 fee** if you had already begun travelling to the pickup.

## 6. Ratings

6.1 Riders rate each completed Ride from 1 to 5. Your displayed rating is the mean of your most
recent 100 rated Rides.

6.2 Thresholds:

| Rolling rating | What happens |
|---|---|
| 4.6 and above | Normal, and eligible for priority allocation |
| 4.2 – 4.59 | Coaching content in the app |
| Below 4.2 | Review, and possible suspension pending improvement |

6.3 A single low rating does not matter. Patterns do. We discount obvious outliers — a 1-star with
no comment, from a Rider who rates every Driver 1 star, is excluded.

## 7. Conduct and safety

7.1 Never exceed the posted speed limit, regardless of what the Rider asks.

7.2 Never carry more passengers than your vehicle is licensed for.

7.3 Report any accident, however minor, through the in-app safety channel the same day.

7.4 Harassment of a Rider in any form results in immediate deactivation and referral to the
authorities where appropriate.

7.5 If you feel unsafe, end the Ride at the nearest safe place and use the emergency button.

---

## 8. Payouts

8.1 **Your share.** You receive **80% of the Fare** on every completed Ride. Cabify-Lite retains
20% as the Platform Fee.

8.2 Tolls, parking and airport entry fees that you pay on the route are reimbursed in full and are
not subject to the Platform Fee.

8.3 **Payout cycle.** Earnings are settled **weekly, every Tuesday**, covering the week ending the
previous Sunday. Funds normally reach the registered bank account the same day.

8.4 **Instant payout.** You may withdraw available earnings on demand for a flat fee of ₹10 per
withdrawal, up to twice per day.

8.5 **Deductions.** Cleaning charge recoveries, negative wallet balances and recovered
overpayments are deducted from the next settlement and itemised in the statement.

8.6 Weekly statements are available in the Driver app under **Earnings → Statements** and are
retained for 24 months.

---

## 9. Support and escalation

9.1 In-app Driver support operates 24 hours.

9.2 Deactivation decisions may be appealed once, within 14 days, through the Driver app. An appeal
is reviewed by a person who was not involved in the original decision.

9.3 Payment queries should be raised with the specific statement week attached. Queries without a
statement reference take materially longer.
