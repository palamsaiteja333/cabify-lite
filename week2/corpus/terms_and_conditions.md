## 4. Booking a Ride

4.1 **Nature of the service.** Cabify-Lite is a technology platform that connects Riders with
independent Drivers. Cabify-Lite does not itself provide transportation services and is not a
transport carrier.

4.2 **Fare estimate.** When you request a Ride, the Platform displays an estimated Fare. The
estimate is based on the expected distance and duration of the journey and on the rates set out in
the Pricing Sheet. The estimate is **not a fixed price quotation**. The Fare actually charged is
calculated on the completed journey.

4.3 **Variation from the estimate.** The final Fare may differ from the estimate where the route
changes at the Rider's request, where the Rider adds a stop, where traffic conditions materially
extend the journey duration, or where waiting charges apply.

4.4 **Acceptance.** A Booking is not confirmed until a Driver accepts it. Until that point, the
Platform is searching and no charge of any kind arises.

4.5 **Allocation.** Rides are allocated to Drivers on the basis of proximity, vehicle type and
availability. Cabify-Lite does not guarantee that a Driver will be available at any particular time
or in any particular location.

4.6 **Stops and route changes.** Additional stops requested in-journey are charged at the
per-minute rate for the duration of the stop, in addition to the per-kilometre rate for any
additional distance.

## 5. Fares and payment

5.1 The Fare is composed of a base fare, a per-kilometre charge, a per-minute charge, and any
applicable surcharges or waiting charges. Current rates are published in the Pricing Sheet, which
forms part of these Terms.

5.2 **Minimum fare.** Every completed Ride is subject to the minimum fare published in the Pricing
Sheet, irrespective of distance or duration.

5.3 **Surge pricing.** At times of high demand the per-kilometre and per-minute components may be
multiplied by a surge factor. Where surge pricing applies, the Platform displays the multiplier
before the Rider confirms the Booking. A Rider who confirms a Booking is deemed to have accepted
the displayed multiplier.

5.4 **Payment methods.** The Platform accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, and the Cabify-Lite
wallet. Cash payment is accepted only where the Driver has opted in and the Platform indicates this
before Booking.

5.5 **Timing of charge.** For card and UPI payments, the Fare is charged on completion of the Ride.
A nominal pre-authorisation may be placed on the payment instrument at the point of Booking; it is
released automatically.

5.6 **Failed payment.** Where a payment fails, the amount owed is added to the Account as an
outstanding balance. New Bookings may be blocked until the balance is cleared. We may charge a
reasonable administrative fee for repeated payment failures.

5.7 **Taxes.** All Fares are inclusive of applicable goods and services tax unless the Platform
states otherwise. A tax invoice is available in the ride history for each completed Ride.

5.8 **Tolls and parking.** Tolls, parking charges and entry fees incurred on the route are passed
through to the Rider at cost and itemised separately on the invoice.
