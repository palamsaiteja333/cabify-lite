# Refunds & Cancellations Policy

**Document owner:** Customer Operations
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective date:** 1 August 2026
**Review cycle:** Every 6 months
**Authority:** This policy is the single authoritative source on cancellation charges and refunds.
Where any other Cabify-Lite material — including the internal Support FAQ, help-centre articles, or
advice given verbally by an agent — differs from this policy, **this policy prevails**.

---

## 1. Scope

This policy covers:

- cancellation of a Booking by the Rider;
- cancellation of a Booking by the Driver;
- refunds arising from cancellations, disputes, service failures and goodwill gestures.

It does **not** cover charges where the Rider fails to present themselves at the pickup point.
Those are governed by the separate **No-Show Policy**, although refunds of a no-show charge, once
approved, are issued under section 5 of this policy.

---

## 2. Cancellation by the Rider

### 2.1 Before a Driver accepts

A Booking that has not yet been accepted by a Driver may be cancelled at any time at **no charge**.
No payment instrument is debited.

### 2.2 The free-cancellation grace period

Once a Driver has accepted the Booking, the Rider has a grace period of **2 minutes** during which
the Booking may be cancelled at no charge.

The grace period runs from the moment of Driver acceptance, not from the moment of Booking.

### 2.3 After the grace period

| When the Rider cancels | Charge |
|---|---|
| Before a Driver accepts | Nil |
| Within 2 minutes of Driver acceptance | Nil |
| More than 2 minutes after acceptance, Driver not yet arrived | **₹30** |
| After the Driver has arrived at the pickup point | **₹30** plus any waiting charge accrued |
| After the journey has commenced | Fare for distance and time travelled, subject to the minimum fare |

### 2.4 Waived cancellation fees

The cancellation fee is **waived** where:

- the Driver is more than 10 minutes later than the estimated arrival time shown at Booking;
- the Driver's recorded position has not moved toward the pickup point for 5 consecutive minutes;
- the vehicle that arrives does not match the vehicle shown in the application;
- the Rider cancels on the Driver's request;
- a severe-weather or civil-disruption advisory is active for the pickup area.

Where a fee has been charged and one of the above applies, the Rider should raise it under
section 6. Waivers are applied as a refund, not as a reversal of the original charge.

---

## 3. Cancellation by the Driver

3.1 Where the Driver cancels an accepted Booking, **the Rider is never charged**. Any
pre-authorisation on the payment instrument is released.

3.2 Where the Driver cancels after arriving at the pickup point and the Rider was present, the
Rider is additionally eligible for a **goodwill credit of ₹50** to the Cabify-Lite wallet, on
request.

3.3 Driver cancellations are monitored. Obligations and thresholds applying to Drivers are set out
in the Driver Handbook.

## 4. Refund eligibility

A refund may be issued in the following circumstances.

| Refund reason | Code used in our systems | Typical amount |
|---|---|---|
| Driver cancelled after the Rider had been charged | `driver_cancelled` | Full |
| Service failure — wrong route, unsafe driving, vehicle condition, overcharge | `service_issue` | Full or partial, on assessment |
| A no-show charge successfully contested | `no_show_contested` | Full |
| Goodwill gesture where no fault is established | `goodwill` | Partial, usually capped at ₹100 |

### 4.1 Partial refunds

Not every refund is a full refund. Where a journey was completed but the service fell short — for
example a materially longer route than necessary — Customer Operations may approve a **partial
refund** representing the difference between the Fare charged and the Fare that should have
applied. Partial refunds are the normal outcome for route and duration disputes.

### 4.2 What is not refundable

- Tolls, parking and entry fees actually incurred.
- The minimum fare on a completed Ride.
- Surge multipliers accepted by the Rider at the point of Booking.
- Cleaning charges where photographic evidence has been assessed and upheld.

---

## 5. Refund timelines

> **5.1 Cabify-Lite issues every approved refund within 7 calendar days of the Ride. No refund is
> issued more than 7 days after the date of the Ride.**

5.2 Refund requests must therefore be received promptly. A request relating to a Ride more than 7
days in the past cannot be processed, because the refund itself could not be issued within the
window stated in 5.1.

5.3 Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Where the original method is no longer
valid, the refund is credited to the Cabify-Lite wallet.

5.4 Once issued, a refund to a card may take a further 3–5 business days to appear on the Rider's
statement, depending on the issuing bank. That bank settlement period is outside our control and is
not counted within the 7 days in 5.1.

5.5 Every refund is recorded against the originating Ride with its reason code, its amount, the
time it was issued, and whether it was issued automatically or by a named agent.

---

## 6. How to request a refund

6.1 Open the Ride in the application, select **Help with this ride**, and choose the reason.

6.2 Supply any supporting evidence at the point of request. A request without evidence takes
longer to assess and is more likely to be declined.

6.3 Requests are acknowledged within 1 business day and decided within 5 business days (see
Terms & Conditions, clause 10.3).

6.4 Automatic refunds — those with `issued_by = auto` in our records — require no request. They
are triggered by the system where the entitlement is unambiguous, principally driver cancellations
after a charge has been taken.

---

## 7. Repeat requests and abuse

7.1 Riders who make a high volume of refund requests relative to their completed Rides are
flagged for manual review. There is no fixed threshold; review is discretionary.

7.2 Where refund requests are found to be systematically false, the Account may be suspended under
clause 3.4 of the Terms & Conditions.
